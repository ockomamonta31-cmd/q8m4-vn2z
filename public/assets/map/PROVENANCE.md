# Dota 2 map background

Map 7.41, © Valve Corporation. Source image:
https://liquipedia.net/dota2/File:Game_map_7.41.jpg
Retrieved from the preserved source export in
https://github.com/whanyu1212/gem-dota/blob/main/assets/maps/Game_map_7.41.jpg
on 2026-10-14. No generated or reconstructed terrain.

Transformation: crop the centered 8356 × 8356 square from the 8878 × 8356
export, then Lanczos resize to 1400 × 1400 and WebP quality 88.
Coordinate calibration: gem-dota src/gem/catalog/map.py (world x 7487–25809,
y 7693–26015). OpenDota cells convert to these units by multiplying by 128.
The 22 tower positions, barracks, ancients and fountains were independently
decoded from Valve replay 9038997807 with server/gc/SpatialReplay.java.
Background covers 7.41; earlier terrain versions are not yet selected.
